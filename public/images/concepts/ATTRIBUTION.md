# Concept panels

These are compressed, uncropped derivatives of existing AI-generated architectural studies in this repository. Each panel is labelled as a concept rather than a photograph of an existing building.

`sources.json` records each original path, SHA-256 and derivative dimensions. Rebuild with `python scripts/build-concept-panels.py`. Original prompts and generation records remain beside the source images under `concepts/`.

All fifteen panels now have assigned concepts. The original five derivatives remain unchanged. Ten missing concepts were generated with built-in `image_gen` on 5 October 2026, using the saved original civic-building prompts and the owner's existing design descriptions for later spaces. Their originals, prompts and generation record are preserved in `concepts/16-concept-garden/`; these illustrations are fictional architectural studies, not evidence of constructed buildings or scientific diagrams.
