# Earlier explorations and Camel Dalí

Images © Livia Zaharia. Retrieved 23 September 2026 for the artist's Livistone project; no general third-party reuse licence is implied.

The five earlier works come from their individual tabs at https://livia.glucosedao.org/pieces/ : First Ring, Moldavian Vault Ring, Art Nouveau Ring, Peas in Pod Ring, and Wormy Ring. These source images include original digital design renders as well as photographs. Archive-estimated dates are explicitly labelled; unknown dates, dimensions and materials remain unasserted. Captions paraphrase the artist's text. Original image paths and SHA-256 hashes are in `src/game/archive-catalogue.json`.

Camel Dalí comes from the exact post supplied by the artist: https://www.instagram.com/p/DdDwLqDlcm2/ . The public post was inspected in the browser and its original image downloaded. Material details (native copper, PLA, leather) were supplied directly by the artist in this task. The enlarged drinking camel and inhabited spaceship cabin are Livistone inventions.

`scripts/extend-archive.py` builds full-size and thumbnail WebP derivatives, preserving image proportions. The cached originals are under ignored `output/testing/research-sources/`. The source photo for Camel Dalí is retrieved through the post's observed image URL; that transient CDN URL is not a runtime dependency.
