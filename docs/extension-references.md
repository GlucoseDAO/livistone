## Train graphics

The two user-supplied Yahoo image links resolved to:

- https://i.pinimg.com/550x/ca/3f/32/ca3f3288c2684ee4ffe7b0f93e1620be.jpg — a Warsaw Metro graphic with sweeping red/teal/cream ribbons and oversized typography.
- https://i.pinimg.com/originals/95/64/6a/95646a932ce8c9a1d0b3d158fab710e6.jpg — a dark Metro 2034 network map with connected stations.

Both were inspected. `transit-art.ts` creates an original campaign using those broad design ideas, with Livistone typography, colors, ring geometry and a science network. The reference images themselves are not shipped as artwork.

## Lake planting

The artist's catalogue records the Untold Galaxy Stage 2017/2018 as intersecting ellipses wrapped with individually extracted textile strips. It credits Mindscape Studio as client. The 2018 diagram was inspected from `/pieces/large-scale-parametrics-Untold%20Galaxy%20stage%202018/2257590444557882.jpg` at https://livia.glucosedao.org/pieces/ .

A Sky for All (Spotlight 2019): https://livia.glucosedao.org/art-design/a-sky-for-all-spotlight-2019 and https://spotlightfestival.ro/en/instalatii/a-sky-for-all . The artist's `/spotlight2019/01.jpg` shows a folded triangular membrane pavilion. The lake sculptures combine split elliptical leaves with folded bracts; they are fictional plants inspired by those structures.

## Future House

Original Camel Dalí post supplied by Livia: https://www.instagram.com/p/DdDwLqDlcm2/ . Material details and architectural direction are the artist's direct instructions. Copper ends use varying teardrop sections with tapered ends; the printed cabin has a separate curved lower hull and a level inhabited deck. Neck and floor meet before the hull threshold. Gallery bays sit under the high portion of the roof.
