LIVISTONE · ART, SCIENCE & NATURE

A town from a jeweller’s imagination

Architect → parametric jewellery designer → citizen scientist

Livia Zaharia began with architecture, moved into parametric jewellery design, and became a citizen scientist. In Livistone, she brings these worlds together: her jewellery pieces become buildings you can walk into, while her machine learning and scientific projects have places of their own.

The background music is Livia herself playing kalimba, recorded live on her phone. You can turn the radio on or off in the menu.
