# Mountain setting, Nanot frame, and jewelry exhibitions — 17 September 2026

This follows the owner's request for textured mountains, a Nanot whose metal carries the enclosure, and real jewelry photographs and information inside all three civic buildings. It extends the approved garden-town direction without replacing the original concept record or extracted STL strands.

- Mountains use one continuous ridged terrain backdrop with a river valley, green foothills, slope-dependent rock exposure, and triplanar scanned rock maps. This replaces the smooth ellipsoid hills. The 1K colour and normal maps are Poly Haven's CC0 Rock Face 03; see `public/textures/mountains/ATTRIBUTION.md`. Mobile reduces the terrain mesh and omits per-pixel normal detail.
- Nanot's original radial hub was unsuitable for an inhabited glass hall. Its preserved outer strand contours are projected *after* curve sampling onto an external envelope. Continuous ribs and connecting rings meet the foundation and carry the visual structure; the glazing sits inside. The frame also supplies collision geometry. This is an architectural interpretation, not an engineering analysis.
- Each civic hall has two freestanding framed photos and a catalogue lectern. All six photographs are authentic Livia studio images, optimized from the same archive used on her live website. Labels contain factual catalogue metadata; E / touch discovery opens an accessible gallery and information table. Fantasy lore stays under a separate heading. Credits and reproduction details are in `public/images/jewelry/ATTRIBUTION.md`.
- Left/Right arrows now turn. A/D strafe; W/S and Up/Down move forward/backward. Mouse rotation still requires a held left-button drag, and remains independent of keyboard turning.

The screenshot script includes external views, all three galleries, the lectern, and the catalogue dialog. Generated checks remain under ignored `output/testing/`. Physical phone performance and Safari/iOS are not yet verified.
