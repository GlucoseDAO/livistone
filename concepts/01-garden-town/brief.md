# Livistone — first concept study

## Confirmed direction

Livistone is a welcoming art-and-science fantasy town inspired by Livia Lore and Livia Zaharia's jewelry. Its architecture has a futuristic clarity: smooth white sculptural surfaces, expressive organic structure, and a sense of lightness reminiscent of Santiago Calatrava. Its intimacy and relationship with nature recall elvish towns, translated into contemporary architecture.

The jewelry's defining shapes must survive their translation into inhabitable buildings. This is a town with rooms, entrances, workshops, gardens, and everyday life.

Fresh green trees grow among the buildings. A modest river passes through the settlement. The overall feeling is cozy, sheltered, and comfortable in both summer and winter.

This phase produces concept images and a design brief. Web navigation, engine selection, and implementation follow discussion of the images.

## Architectural language

- Smooth warm-white shells and branching structural ribs establish the town's shared identity.
- Silver-colored metal, brass, amber-colored glass, smoky amethyst glass, and walnut-toned timber retain the artifacts' material identities.
- Buildings have legible human-scale doors, occupied floors, enclosed rooms, and accessible garden paths.
- Mature deciduous trees shade summer routes and allow winter light into gardens. Evergreen planting shelters exposed edges.
- Deep eaves, covered walks, recessed entries, glazed winter gardens, and intimate courtyards express the requested seasonal comfort. These are visual design intentions, not verified building-performance claims.
- Soft planted riverbanks and low bridges connect compact groups of buildings. Greenery occupies the spaces between landmarks as well as their surroundings.
- Stone paving and timber touches temper the white architecture; interiors glow gently rather than emitting neon light.

## Artifact sources and confirmed civic roles

The source descriptions and photographs are in the adjacent `livia` repository. The user assigned the three civic roles below to Livistone; they extend the source lore. Architectural details remain concept proposals.

| Artifact | Source identity | Proposed building translation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nut of Power | Supreme artifact controlling the others; a spherical form joining walnut shell and amethyst, held by brass | City Hall: two complementary shell halves, walnut textures, a softly translucent crystal volume, and visible brass connections |
| Mitoring / Mitochondria Ring | Amber held within folded silver contours; energy and endurance | Ministry of Energy: an oval inhabited building with warm amber glazing, a winter garden, and looping ribs that retain the ring's distinctive contours |
| Nanot of Power | A spherical, open silver lattice with angular folding strands; nanotechnology and biotechnology | Ministry of Science: the actual irregular lattice language wrapped around enclosed laboratories, public learning rooms, and sheltered planting |

The Nanot should not become a generic triangular geodesic dome. Mitoring should retain the irregular folds around its amber center. The Nut should retain its joined half-shell construction rather than becoming a generic fantasy palace.
