# Integrated town and Mycelium correction — 18 September 2026

The user rejected the separated Living Waters scene and clarified that “umbrella” referred to the mushroom-like folded crown of the Mycelium ring, not a literal parasol. This correction supersedes the earlier remote placement recorded in `concepts/10-rail-gardens/`; that record remains historical.

Living Waters now joins the north of the civic district at `(0, -110)`, with two continuous walking approaches from City Hall and Glucose Commons. All landmarks share the scene, terrain, Rapier world and map. Embryo Station is placed on the southern bank with its ring exit facing the LIVISTONE gateway and bridge. Spawn is just outside the exit at `(0, 58)`. Authored station geometry remains unchanged internally; one rigid transform also places its posters, interactions and collisions.

The user's follow-up rejected the unnecessary northern platform, duplicate train, western railway loop and the circular mountain wall surrounding an empty flat field. All rail facilities now belong to the southern station: its single train and platform, with two main guideways and the existing east/west mountain bores. The train remains a boardable parked interior; the excursion state machine is removed. Every garden is reached on foot within the same town.

The terrain is one continuous surface. Unequal elongated ridges, low saddles and river valleys replace the radial mountain ring. Wooded foothills rise from the town’s edges, and trees taper up their slopes. The walking grid shares the same elevation field. Tunnel approaches and far exits are graded and cut through the real hillside triangles.

The two checked source images are `public/images/jewelry/catalogue/mycelium-1.webp` and `mycelium-2.webp`. They show repeated curled, pierced silver folds wrapping an opal. The procedural grove uses those features: open metallic mushroom crowns, varied tall branching stems and opalescent centers. It retains the opal-drainage story, atmospheric rain, visible rill and basin. Crown geometry and density are reduced on mobile, with full path clearance in both tiers. No generated reference imagery or replacement jewellery assets were needed.

Validation: the production build and all 40 unit tests passed; all 27 desktop/touch browser scenarios passed across the final verification runs. Railway tests were rerun after tunnel grading and the final aperture correction. A 36-view landmark capture was generated, with the town layout, station/platform, arrival, grove and tunnel approaches visually reviewed.
