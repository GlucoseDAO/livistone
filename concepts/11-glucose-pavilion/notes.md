# Glucose Commons — molecular research pavilion

18 September 2026. User request: extend the implementation plan with one building about Livia's glucose-prediction work; consider glucose or insulin coordinates from PDB as architecture/monument, with GlucoseDAO achievement posters and repository links.

## Built interpretation

An open pavilion on the eastern station garden path, centered at `(38, -40)`, with a 19.2 m circular limestone floor and a clear north–south passage. Two broad overhead ribbons preserve the A/B alpha-carbon traces of human insulin from **1TRZ**. Sage silver and warm white distinguish the chains; three gold connections locate the deposited disulfide bonds. Fine gold arms follow the corresponding cysteine CA–CB–SG coordinates back to the ribbons. Curved supports join the fold to the floor, leaving the central route open. The entire molecular trace is lifted above walking headroom. A separate small carbon/oxygen model of **GLC, alpha-D-glucopyranose**, stands in a southern side alcove.

Six freestanding planar posters face inward from the perimeter. Each opens a readable, scrollable discovery with ordinary HTTPS links, using E, the touch interaction prompt or a short scene click. Looking and walking keep their existing controls. Sources can be reopened from the saved journal. The pavilion is a fifth map landmark, independent of the three jewelry exhibition halls.

The public records are scientific references, not Livia jewelry. The architecture, architectural scale and symbolic materials are new Livistone fiction. The molecular extraction retains source hashes and can be regenerated offline: [source attribution](../../data/molecules/README.md).
